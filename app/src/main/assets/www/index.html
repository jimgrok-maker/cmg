What is CMG?

A trip planner, not a chartplotter. It estimates course made good from polars + forecast wind and keeps you inside a coarse shoreline. Always check NOAA / CHS for depths.

TWD FROM vs the arrow

Numbers are meteorological FROM (where the wind comes from). Gold barbs point downwind (where it is going). Tap a barb for both.

Where does the wind come from?

Open-Meteo. Default HRRR is the U.S. high-res model (~3 km / 1.6 nm cells, hourly, ~48 h). GFS and ECMWF are coarser backups. Manual is whatever you type.

Does the wind change along a 10-hour sail?

Yes, two ways. Time: each hour of the trip reads that hour of the forecast. Place: after the first rough route, CMG pulls HRRR at about 8–12 points along the track (roughly every hour of sailing) and reruns the polar with the nearest cell. A 10-mile hop barely notices. A 40–50 nm Erie or Bay run can change FROM angle at the far end.

Two-pass routing

Pass 1 uses map-center wind to guess where you will be each hour. Pass 2 fetches those cells and rebuilds speed and ETA. If the second fetch fails, the center forecast stays.

What it will not fix

Model error, gusts, and thunderstorm outflow. Spatial sampling only stops us from using Sandusky’s 14:00 wind when the bow is off Pelee at 18:00.

Polars and speed

Working-sail synthetic polars scaled to hull speed (1.34 × √LWL). MacGregor 26X assumes board down, full ballast. Avg kn in the header is polar CMG for the plan. SOG kn is GPS over ground.

Motor policy

Default is sail only. Hybrid compare motors at 4 kn when VMG is under 2 kn. Goal is 4 kn plus when the angle allows.

Islands

Erie has keep-off hulls for Pelee, Middle Island, Bass, Kelleys. A reaching rhumb that crosses land gets a short wet detour. Other lakes use a coarse shoreline only — add taps around points.

Colors

Green reach, yellow tight, red no-go, blue motor. Gold line is the GPS wake, not the plan.

Units

Nautical miles and knots. Courses true unless Magnetic is checked (local variation west).

P50 / P90

P50 is polar time in the forecast we have. P90 is that time × 1.22 — a fudge for lulls, bad trim, and traffic, not a second weather model.
